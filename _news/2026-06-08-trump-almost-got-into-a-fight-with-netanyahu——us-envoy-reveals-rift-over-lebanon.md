US Ambassador to Lebanon Michel Issa revealed that President Donald Trump came close to a direct confrontation with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu over Israel's strikes on Lebanon. Speaking to reporters after a meeting with Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri, Issa said Trump follows Lebanon's situation daily and made clear that Washington does not want the conflict to expand further.

Issa described both the Israeli airstrike on Beirut's suburbs and Iran's missile response as a "political message," adding that the United States has actively decided to prevent the confrontation from escalating beyond its current scope. The remarks suggest a meaningful gap between the Trump administration and Netanyahu's government on how far military action in the region should go.
